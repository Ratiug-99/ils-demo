A click-through copy of the draft with its sample data. Every screen and tab works as a page; tick each one off as you go. Buttons that would save show a short note instead.

The register: five screens

  1. Dashboard

    How complete the support package is, what needs attention, predicted against observed figures.

  2. Configuration: positions

    The motor bogie as a tree. Analyse marks a position as a candidate for the analysis.

  3. Configuration: parts catalogue

    Every part with its supplier, cost and lead time.

  4. Configuration: supplier choice

    Alternative parts side by side, including the rejected brake-pad supplier.

  5. One part: axle box bearing BRG-130

    The part page: where it is fitted, its failure modes, repair, task and spares, with sources and revisions.

  6. Failures and hazards

    Failure modes with predicted and observed rates kept apart; hazards wait for a named person.

  7. Repair options

    Options per position with cost and turnaround, and the decision a person made.

  8. Task library

    Maintenance tasks, with one printable task sheet: T-006.

  9. Provisioning pack

    Spares quantities worked out from the figures, and what goes into the delivery pack.

VisBo, the agent

  1. Import a spreadsheet

    Pick one of the sample files: VisBo reads it and opens the result it made.

  2. An import that needs youneeds you

    A German supplier FMEA. VisBo is unsure about one column: try Accept or Change. Import waits until a person answers.

  3. A newer version of a known file

    The layout is remembered; the changes against the earlier file are listed: 2 new, 3 changed, 1 gone.

  4. A file with a note to the AI

    A door BOM with a planted note telling the AI to approve everything. VisBo flags it and only made drafts.

  5. Datasheet to draft

    A yaw damper datasheet: every value shows the line it came from, and line 18 (an instruction to AI tools) is flagged, not followed.

  6. What is missing

    Gaps that block the support package, per assembly.

  7. Ask VisBo

    The Ask VisBo button at the top of every page: a short thread with a summary first, grouped answers and the records used. In this copy the prepared questions answer.

Records

  1. Review queue

    Drafts waiting for a second person, grouped by where they came from.

  2. Audit trail

    Who did what and when, VisBo included.

In this copy

  • Everything is invented sample data, the same as in the draft app.
  • Two sample files are already imported (a trailer bogie and a door system), so their drafts sit in the register and the review queue next to the motor bogie.
  • Nothing saves: Approve, Import, Save and the name picker show a note.
  • You work as Reviewer B (sample), so Approve shows on what Analyst A wrote.
  • Downloads and your own uploads work only in the draft app.
  • Open the screens on a laptop: the draft is built for 1366 px and wider. This list works on a phone; the screens do not yet.
