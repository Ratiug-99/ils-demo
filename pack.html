The delivery pack holds approved records only, for the whole register (all assemblies), with where each value came from. Excel for people, JSON for systems.
Stock = the smallest quantity at which a random demand finds a spare on the shelf with the target probability (fill rate, Poisson over the pipeline time), plus the planned replacements that fall in the pipeline time; a planner can override it with a reason. A part with a missing rate or mixed repair routes is not calculated. Demand uses latest revisions. Not modelled in v0: lead-time variability, repair scrap rate, wear-based replacement (except via preventive tasks), stock locations. Live stock balances belong in the maintenance or ERP system.
