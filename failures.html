Severity and likelihood are the analyst's assessment, not calculated from failure rates. The system records the acceptance; it does not give it.

HZ-001

Axle journal failure leading to derailment

approved r1
Tolerable Catastrophic Improbable

Bearing overheats and is not found before it fails; any speed.

Caused by
FM-001 · Bearing overheats or seizes
FM-014 · Brush worn out or poor contact
Evidence
Bearing qualification report; TCMS alarm specification (sample refs)
Acceptance
proposed
CONTROLS
1. Bearing temperature alarm with stop-train instruction (TCMS).
2. Earthing brush length check at every B.
3. Bearing unit replaced after any confirmed overheat.
